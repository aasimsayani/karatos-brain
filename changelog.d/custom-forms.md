- Custom forms connector (`@karatos/connectors`): imports five years of appointment requests, repair intake and custom order requests from Typeform forms, then reads new submissions with an overlap of five minutes, paging Typeform's Responses API back with `before` 1000 at a time, two requests a second, on the US or either EU host. The client only GETs a form's definition and its responses with parameters it checks, and declares no writes. Any other form tool or website form can post submissions signed with HMAC-SHA256 over a timestamp and the raw body (`verifyFormWebhook`, five-minute window either way), and `verifyTypeformWebhook` checks `Typeform-Signature`. Both go through a form mapping profile (`parseFormProfile`, generic profile built in) into one `forms.submission_received` event that keeps only safe fields: the kind of request, requested date and time, item type, metal, stone, budget, preferred contact channel and store. Typed answers are kept only when they match a profile value. Names, emails, phone numbers, addresses, descriptions, hidden fields and file links are never kept; a customer becomes a reference keyed with the new `CUSTOMER_REF_KEY`. The scheduled sync runs it when `custom_forms` is in `ENABLED_INTEGRATIONS`.
- Connector test kit: a Typeform mock built from its docs, with synthetic responses that carry the personal data a real one would.
- Nightly sandbox run: a Typeform check, which runs once the `SANDBOX_CUSTOM_FORMS_TYPEFORM_*` secrets are set.
- Secrets manifest: `TYPEFORM_ACCESS_TOKEN`, `TYPEFORM_FORM_IDS`, `TYPEFORM_API_HOST`, `TYPEFORM_WEBHOOK_SECRET`, `CUSTOMER_REF_KEY` and `FORMS_MAPPING_PROFILE`; `WEBHOOK_SIGNING_SECRET` now also verifies signed form submissions.
