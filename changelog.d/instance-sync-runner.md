- Instances now sync their connectors on a schedule. Every built connector listed in `ENABLED_INTEGRATIONS` that has its settings runs one at a time, its historical import first and then incremental syncs, every `SYNC_INTERVAL_MINUTES` (default 60) after the last round ends. One connector failing is logged without stopping the others, and integrations it can't sync are logged by the settings they're missing, never their values. `ENABLED_INTEGRATIONS` is now checked against the catalog at startup, and the image now includes `@karatos/connectors`
