- RapNet and Rapaport Price List connector (`@karatos/connectors`): reads the weekly Rapaport Price List for round and pear diamonds, storing each new list as `diamond_price.list_published` with every cell's carat range, color, clarity, price per carat and pricing date, and the cells that moved in it as `diamond_price.list_changed` with their old prices. It signs in machine to machine to Rapaport's fixed token address, renews the token before it expires, sends only GETs to the two price list endpoints, and dead-letters a list with a cell that doesn't read. Instant Inventory listings aren't copied, as Rapaport's terms forbid it. Turn it on with `rapnet` in `ENABLED_INTEGRATIONS` and the `RAPNET_*` settings in `secrets/manifest.json`.
- Connector test kit: a Rapaport mock built from RapTech's developer pages, covering the token service and both price list endpoints, with a made-up price list row builder.
