- BigCommerce connector (`@karatos/connectors`): reads five years of orders (with their lines and refunds), products and variants, stock at every location, customers and B2B price lists with each variant's price and quantity tiers, then every change on the sync schedule. Sales, returns, products, stock and customers arrive as the same events the Shopify, Square and Clover connectors send (`order.updated`, `sale.completed`, `sale.returned`, `product.updated`, `inventory.counted`, `customer.profile_updated`); price lists use the new neutral `price_list.updated` and `price_list.price_updated`. BigCommerce can't report a token's scopes, so the client sends only GET requests to nine listed paths and first checks the token belongs to the configured store. Products, customers and orders are paged by change time so a record edited mid-pass can't make another be skipped. Rate limits are waited out from `X-Rate-Limit-Requests-Left` and `X-Rate-Limit-Time-Reset-Ms`, then the sync resumes from its checkpoint. Customers are kept only as references keyed with `CUSTOMER_REF_KEY`; no names, contact details, addresses, IP addresses or notes are kept. Turn it on with `bigcommerce` in `ENABLED_INTEGRATIONS` and the `BIGCOMMERCE_*` settings in `secrets/manifest.json`.
- `verifyBigCommerceWebhook` checks a shared secret header set when each webhook is created, in constant time, as BigCommerce store webhooks are unsigned; `bigCommerceWebhookChange` drops notifications for other stores or older than 49 hours, and `BigCommerceWebhookDeduper` takes each event hash once. Notifications only prompt a sync.
- Connector test kit: a BigCommerce mock built from BigCommerce's docs, with rate-limit headers, RFC 2822 v2 times and 204 empty pages, and fixture builders for each record type.
- Nightly sandbox run: a BigCommerce check, which runs once the `SANDBOX_BIGCOMMERCE_*` secrets are set.
- Secrets manifest: `BIGCOMMERCE_STORE_HASH`, `BIGCOMMERCE_ACCESS_TOKEN`, `BIGCOMMERCE_WEBHOOK_SECRET` and `CUSTOMER_REF_KEY`.
