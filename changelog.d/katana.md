- Katana connector (`@karatos/connectors`): imports five years of manufacturing orders, then each one changed since the last sync, and counts stock for every product and material at each location. Orders become the same `manufacturing.job_updated` events PIRO gives, with status, quantities, deadlines, costs and whether their materials are in stock; stock becomes `inventory.counted`. Order notes, serial numbers and custom fields aren't kept. Katana's API keys can't be limited to reading, so the client only sends GET requests to the manufacturing orders and inventory lists; creating a manufacturing order is declared behind the write gate and turned down. Turn it on with `katana` in `ENABLED_INTEGRATIONS` and `KATANA_API_KEY`.
- Connector test kit: a Katana mock built from its docs, with manufacturing order and inventory fixture builders.
