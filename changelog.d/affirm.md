- Affirm connector (`@karatos/connectors`): imports five years of Affirm financed orders with their current status, refunds, disputes and daily settlements, then keeps them current, reading each older transaction again when a capture, void or refund changes it. Orders, refunds and disputes arrive as the same neutral events Stripe's do, linked to the transaction and the store's order number, and each day's settlement becomes a payout with its sales, refunds and fees. An Affirm key pair can't be limited to reading, so the client sends its five listed GET reads only, with parameters it checked, and throws before sending anything else; capture, refund and void are declared behind the write gate. No shopper names, emails, phone numbers or addresses are kept. `verifyAffirmWebhook` checks `Affirm-Signature` (HMAC-SHA512, `v0` only, five-minute tolerance). Turn it on with `affirm` in `ENABLED_INTEGRATIONS` and the `AFFIRM_*` settings in `secrets/manifest.json`. US accounts only for now.
- Connector test kit: an Affirm mock built from its docs, with fixture builders for transactions, transaction events, settlements and disputes.
- Nightly sandbox run: an Affirm check, which runs once the `SANDBOX_AFFIRM_*` secrets are set.
