- Bank and credit card statements connector (`@karatos/connectors`): reads CSV exports from any bank, one folder per account under `BANK_STATEMENTS_IMPORT_DIR`, through a parsing profile (the built-in generic one, or a client's own per-account profiles named by `BANK_STATEMENTS_PROFILES`). It finds the header below a bank's title block, reads one signed amount or separate debit and credit columns, and sorts each transaction into cash deposits, card settlements, vendor payments, payroll, fees or other for the finance department. A transaction is keyed by account, date, amount and its count among identical ones that day, so overlapping exports, and the PDF statements coming next, store it once. Six years of history, read-only, no writes declared
- File-based connectors share one read loop (`importFiles`): only new or changed files are read, a crash resumes mid-file, and files that leave the folder drop out of the checkpoint. The POS connector now uses it, and its column matching moved to shared code
