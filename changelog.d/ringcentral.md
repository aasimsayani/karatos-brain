- RingCentral connector (`@karatos/connectors`): imports two years of calls, texts and voicemail on the store's RingCentral line, then rereads from where the last pass started, less a day, on each sync. They arrive as the same `communication.call_logged` and `communication.message_logged` events Quo's and Twilio's give, with missed calls, durations, and whether a text or voicemail was read. It signs in with a JWT credential under an app given only Read Call Log and Read Messages, and stops if the token can do more; sending a text and placing a call are declared behind the write gate and turned down. No text, voicemail audio, transcripts, recordings or caller names are kept, and customers' numbers become the same customer references Quo, custom forms, Klaviyo and AvaTax make. Turn it on with `ringcentral` in `ENABLED_INTEGRATIONS`, `RINGCENTRAL_CLIENT_ID`, `RINGCENTRAL_CLIENT_SECRET`, `RINGCENTRAL_JWT` and `CUSTOMER_REF_KEY`.
- Connector test kit: a RingCentral mock built from its developer guide, with call, text and voicemail fixture builders.
