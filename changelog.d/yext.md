- Yext connector (`@karatos/connectors`): imports two years of reviews from Google, Yelp, Facebook and the other sites Yext watches, with the rating, an excerpt, whether the store has replied and a link to answer it, and each location's name, address, time zone and whether it is closed. Its only API requests are two GETs, it keeps no reviewer name or email, and it masks email addresses and phone numbers in what a review says. Replying to a review and changing a location's hours are declared behind the write gate, turned off, needing approval. Turn it on with `yext` in `ENABLED_INTEGRATIONS` and `YEXT_API_KEY`, plus `YEXT_ACCOUNT_ID` and `YEXT_ENVIRONMENT` when needed.
- Connector test kit: a Yext Management API v2 mock built from Yext's docs, with fixture builders for locations and reviews.
- Nightly sandbox run: a Yext check against a sandbox account, which runs once `SANDBOX_YEXT_API_KEY` is set.
