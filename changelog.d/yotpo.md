- Yotpo connector (`@karatos/connectors`): imports five years of a store's product and site reviews, then the reviews changed in the last two days on each sync, and, when the Loyalty GUID and API key are given, the Loyalty members updated in the last seven days. Reviews become the same `review.updated` events Yext and Birdeye give, with the rating, product, sentiment, whether the reviewer was verified or incentivized, and an excerpt and title with email addresses and phone numbers masked. Members become `loyalty.customer_updated` events with the points balance, points earned, perks redeemed, VIP tier and spend. Reviewers and members become keyed customer references; names, birthdays and referral links aren't kept. Yotpo's keys can't be limited to reading, so the client sends only a token request and two GETs; replying to a review and adjusting points are declared behind the write gate and turned down. Turn it on with `yotpo` in `ENABLED_INTEGRATIONS`, `YOTPO_STORE_ID`, `YOTPO_API_SECRET` and `CUSTOMER_REF_KEY`, plus `YOTPO_LOYALTY_GUID` and `YOTPO_LOYALTY_API_KEY` for Loyalty.
- Connector test kit: a Yotpo mock built from its docs, with review and Loyalty member fixture builders.
