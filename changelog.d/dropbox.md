- Dropbox connector (read-only): the shared folder's files and folders as `document.file_updated` and `document.file_removed` events, the same ones Google Drive gives, with each file's kind, size, times, revision and content hash. The historical import lists five years; later syncs read Dropbox's change feed, so edits, moves and deletions are seen without listing again. Paths are kept only as hashed references, and names can be left out. `dropboxFileSource` reads one folder's files with their contents, checked against Dropbox's content hash, for the statement and invoice imports to read from next. Every token is asked for read scopes only and the client stops if Dropbox reports more. Webhook signatures are checked with the app secret. Saving a report is declared behind the write gate and turned down.
