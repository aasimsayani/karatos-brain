- RFID and barcode tray counts connector (read-only): reads the count exports reader and scanner software saves as CSV, one row per tag, and turns each tray or case count into an `inventory.tray_counted` event with what was found, what was missing and what was in the wrong tray. The inventory department flags a tray whose latest count came up short, names the missing tags and adds up their cost where it knows the SKU, and points out pieces in the wrong tray.
