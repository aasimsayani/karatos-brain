- ShipStation connector (`@karatos/connectors`): imports two years of shipments and labels from ShipStation's API v2, then the shipments changed since the last sync and the last 30 days of labels on each sync. Shipments become `shipment.updated` events with the status, carrier and service, confirmation, insurance provider and insured value next to what the customer paid (with how far it falls short), the order, skus and item count, and the destination's state and country. Labels become the same `shipment.tracked` events the UPS connector gives, with the tracking number, a shared status, costs, insured value and whether the label was voided. Names, addresses, phone numbers, email addresses and buyer notes aren't kept. ShipStation has no read-only key, so the client sends only GETs of the two lists; buying a label and adding tracking are declared behind the write gate and turned down. Turn it on with `shipstation` in `ENABLED_INTEGRATIONS` and `SHIPSTATION_API_KEY`.
- Connector test kit: a ShipStation mock built from its docs, with shipment and label fixtures.
