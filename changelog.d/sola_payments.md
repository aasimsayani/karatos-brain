- Sola Payments connector (`@karatos/connectors`): imports five years of a gateway's card and check payments and refunds from Sola's transaction report, then the last two days again on each sync. They become the same `payment.charge_updated` and `payment.refund_updated` events Stripe and Authorize.net give, with the amount, card brand, approval, void and batch. Customers become keyed customer references; card numbers, tokens, names and invoices aren't kept. Sola's API keys can't be limited to reports, so the client posts only `Report:All` to Sola's reporting address and never the gateway; creating payment links and recurring schedules are declared behind the write gate and turned down. Turn it on with `sola_payments` in `ENABLED_INTEGRATIONS`, `SOLA_API_KEY` and `CUSTOMER_REF_KEY`, plus `SOLA_TIME_ZONE` when the account isn't on Eastern time.
- Connector test kit: a Sola Payments mock built from its docs, with a report row fixture builder.
- Nightly sandbox run: a Sola Payments check, which runs once the `SANDBOX_SOLA_PAYMENTS_API_KEY` secret is set.
