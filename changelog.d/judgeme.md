- Judge.me connector (`@karatos/connectors`): imports five years of a shop's product and store reviews, then the reviews posted or changed in the last two days on each sync. Reviews become the same `review.updated` events Yext, Birdeye and Yotpo give, with the rating, product, photos, verification and publish status, an excerpt and title with email addresses and phone numbers masked, and reviews of two stars or fewer marked for follow-up. Reviewers become keyed customer references; names and IP addresses aren't kept. The client sends only GETs of the review list; replying to a review and publishing or hiding one are declared behind the write gate and turned down. Turn it on with `judgeme` in `ENABLED_INTEGRATIONS`, `JUDGEME_SHOP_DOMAIN`, `JUDGEME_API_TOKEN` and `CUSTOMER_REF_KEY`.
- Connector test kit: a Judge.me mock built from its docs, with a review fixture builder.
