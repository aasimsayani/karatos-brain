- WooCommerce connector: reads a store's products with their variations and stock, orders, refunds and customers over the REST API v3, the whole catalog and five years of orders and refunds, then every change on the sync schedule. Sales, returns, products and stock arrive as the same events the Shopify, Square and Clover connectors send (`order.updated`, `sale.completed`, `sale.returned`, `product.updated`, `inventory.counted`), with the order's payment and each refund in the Stripe connector's shape. It sends GET requests only, to five listed endpoints, with a Read key in the Authorization header over https; WooCommerce has no way to ask a key's permission without a write, so the owner creates a Read key and a Write-only key is stopped on the first read. The store address must be https and a public hostname that resolves to public addresses only, and redirects are never followed. Orders, products and refunds are paged from the newest change on each page, so nothing is skipped while the store changes. Customers become references keyed with `CUSTOMER_REF_KEY`; no names, contact details, addresses, notes, IP addresses or user agents are kept. Turn it on with `woocommerce` in `ENABLED_INTEGRATIONS` and the `WOOCOMMERCE_*` settings in `secrets/manifest.json`.
- `verifyWooCommerceWebhook` checks `X-WC-Webhook-Signature` over the raw body in constant time, and `WooCommerceReplayGuard` handles each delivery ID once; since WooCommerce signs no timestamp, a delivery of a record last changed more than a day ago gives no events.
- Connector test kit: a WooCommerce mock built from WooCommerce's docs, with key permissions, page headers and a store clock behind GMT, and fixture builders for each record type.
- Nightly sandbox run: a WooCommerce check, which runs once the `SANDBOX_WOOCOMMERCE_*` secrets are set.
- Secrets manifest: `WOOCOMMERCE_STORE_URL`, `WOOCOMMERCE_CONSUMER_KEY`, `WOOCOMMERCE_CONSUMER_SECRET`, `WOOCOMMERCE_WEBHOOK_SECRET` and `CUSTOMER_REF_KEY`.
