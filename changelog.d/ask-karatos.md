- Added asking the instance a question in plain words: `POST /v1/ask` with `{"question": "..."}`, or `GET /v1/ask?q=`, answers "what sold this week?", "which payouts are late?", "what's low on stock?", "who is waiting on a reply?", "what's waiting on me?" and "is everything syncing?" from synced data only. Every number names the event ids and systems it was counted from. Questions are matched by their words, with no language model, and periods ("today", "yesterday", "this week", "last week", "last 7 days", "this month", "last month", a month, a day or a range of days) are read as whole days in `STORE_TIME_ZONE`, so a day is 23 or 25 hours long when the clocks change. A question it can't match gets "I can't answer that yet" with the list of what it can answer, a period it can't read is said to be unreadable rather than guessed, and a question about data that hasn't synced gets "nothing has synced for that yet" instead of zeros. Questions are 1 to 500 characters; the instance key is needed and the support key gets 403. Read-only. See docs/ask.md.
