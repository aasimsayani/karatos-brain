- Lightspeed Retail connector (X-Series, read-only): imports five years of sales and returns from every outlet, with every product, stock level and customer, then keeps them current by reading what changed since the last version it reached. Sales, returns, products, stock and customers arrive as the same events Square's do, so the sales, inventory and clienteling departments read either POS the same way, and customers are linked by a keyed reference with no names, emails, phone numbers, birthdays or addresses kept. It connects with the four read scopes only, checks the scopes Lightspeed reports on every token and stops on any other, and keeps the newest refresh token encrypted in the instance's database. Adjusting stock is declared behind the write gate and turned down. Turn it on with `lightspeed` in `ENABLED_INTEGRATIONS` and the `LIGHTSPEED_*` settings in `secrets/manifest.json`.
- Connector test kit: a Lightspeed X-Series mock built from its docs, with fixture builders for products, inventory records, customers and sales.
- Nightly sandbox run: a Lightspeed check against a trial store, which runs once its `SANDBOX_LIGHTSPEED_*` secrets are set.
