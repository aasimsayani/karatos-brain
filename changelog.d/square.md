- Square connector: reads catalog items, inventory counts, customers, orders, payments and refunds from every location, five years back, then every change on the sync schedule, pinned to Square API version 2026-09-16. Sales, returns, items and stock arrive as the same events the Shopify and POS connectors send (`sale.completed`, `sale.returned`, `product.updated`, `inventory.counted`), with SKUs looked up from the catalog, and payments and refunds in the Stripe connector's shape. It is read-only twice over: it reads eight listed endpoints only, and it checks the token's scopes with Square and stops on anything beyond the six read scopes, so a full-access personal token won't do. Rate limits are backed off with jitter and resumed from the checkpoint. Customers become references keyed with `CUSTOMER_REF_KEY`; no names, contact details, notes or card digits are kept, only a card's brand. Turn it on with `square` in `ENABLED_INTEGRATIONS` and the `SQUARE_*` settings in `secrets/manifest.json`.
- `verifySquareWebhook` checks `x-square-hmacsha256-signature` over the notification URL and raw body, and `SquareReplayGuard` handles each event ID once, since Square signs no timestamp.
- Connector test kit: a Square mock built from Square's docs, with OAuth refresh, token status scopes and query-bound cursors, and fixture builders for each record type.
- Nightly sandbox run: a Square check, which runs once the `SANDBOX_SQUARE_*` secrets are set.
- Secrets manifest: `SQUARE_APPLICATION_ID`, `SQUARE_APPLICATION_SECRET`, `SQUARE_REFRESH_TOKEN`, `SQUARE_ACCESS_TOKEN`, `SQUARE_ENVIRONMENT`, `SQUARE_WEBHOOK_SIGNATURE_KEY`, `SQUARE_WEBHOOK_URL` and `CUSTOMER_REF_KEY`.
