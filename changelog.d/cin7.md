- Cin7 Core connector (`@karatos/connectors`): imports every product, customer and supplier and five years of sales and purchases from Cin7 Core's API v2, then what changed on each sync, and the stock at each location every time. Products become `product.updated` events, customers `customer.profile_updated`, suppliers `accounting.vendor_updated`, sales `order.updated` and purchases `accounting.purchase_order_updated`, with totals, what is paid and what is still owed in cents. Stock becomes `inventory.counted` for each product at each location, bin and batch. Addresses, contacts, phone numbers and email addresses aren't kept. Cin7 Core's docs describe no way to limit an API application, so the client sends only GETs of the six lists, spaced a second apart to stay inside 60 calls a minute; adjusting stock and creating a purchase order are declared behind the write gate and turned down. Turn it on with `cin7` in `ENABLED_INTEGRATIONS`, `CIN7_ACCOUNT_ID` and `CIN7_APPLICATION_KEY`.
- Connector test kit: a Cin7 Core mock built from its docs, with product, customer, supplier, sale, purchase and stock fixtures.
