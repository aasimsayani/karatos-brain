- Authorize.net connector (`@karatos/connectors`): imports five years of settlement batches and the transactions in them, walking the settled batch list in 31-day windows, then keeps them current along with every unsettled transaction. Refunds, voids, chargebacks and returned eChecks arrive as the same neutral events Stripe's do, linked to the charge, and each settlement batch becomes a payout. An Authorize.net key can't be limited to reading, so the client sends Transaction Reporting requests only and throws before sending anything else. Cards are kept as their brand only, with no names, emails, addresses or customer IPs. `verifyAuthorizeNetWebhook` checks `X-ANET-Signature`. Turn it on with `authorize_net` in `ENABLED_INTEGRATIONS` and the `AUTHORIZE_NET_*` settings in `secrets/manifest.json`.
- Connector test kit: an Authorize.net mock built from its docs, with fixture builders for batches, transactions and transaction details.
- Nightly sandbox run: an Authorize.net check, which runs once the `SANDBOX_AUTHORIZE_NET_*` secrets are set.
