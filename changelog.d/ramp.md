- Ramp connector (`@karatos/connectors`): imports five years of company card transactions, then each one Ramp says changed since the last sync. They become `card.transaction_updated` events with the amount, merchant, spend category, GL account, receipt and approval state; cardholders are kept as their Ramp user ID, never a name, and memos and receipts as whether there is one. The app asks for `transactions:read` only and a token granted any other scope isn't used; adding a memo and coding a transaction are declared behind the write gate and turned down. Turn it on with `ramp` in `ENABLED_INTEGRATIONS`, `RAMP_CLIENT_ID` and `RAMP_CLIENT_SECRET`.
- Connector test kit: a Ramp mock built from its docs, with a card transaction fixture builder.
- Nightly sandbox run: a Ramp check, which runs once the `SANDBOX_RAMP_CLIENT_ID` and `SANDBOX_RAMP_CLIENT_SECRET` secrets are set.
