- IDEX Online connector (`@karatos/connectors`): reads the business's IDEX Onsite feed, natural stones and optionally lab-grown ones (`IDEX_FEEDS`), and records each stone that is new or changed as `diamond_listing.listed`, with grades, measurements and its asking price after the business's markup, and each one that leaves the feed as `diamond_listing.delisted`. A stone that comes back is recorded again. The secret is sent only to IDEX's fixed feed addresses, files over 200 MB or 50,000 stones (`IDEX_MAX_STONES`) stop the sync, and an empty file never delists the feed. Media links aren't stored. Turn it on with `idex` in `ENABLED_INTEGRATIONS` and the `IDEX_*` settings in `secrets/manifest.json`.
- Connector test kit: an IDEX mock built from IDEX's API pages, with both feeds' header rows and a synthetic stone builder. A doc mock route can now answer a text body as written, such as a CSV file.
