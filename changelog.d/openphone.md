- Quo (formerly OpenPhone) connector (`@karatos/connectors`): imports two years of calls and texts on a workspace's Quo lines, then rereads from where the last pass started, less a day, on each sync, since Quo lists by creation time only. They arrive as the same `communication.call_logged` and `communication.message_logged` events Twilio's give, with missed calls, durations, who answered and delivery status. Quo keys have full access, so the client sends only List calls and List messages; sending a text and adding a contact note are declared behind the write gate and turned down. No message text, media, summaries, voicemails, recordings or transcripts are kept, and customers' numbers become the same customer references custom forms, Klaviyo and AvaTax make. Turn it on with `openphone` in `ENABLED_INTEGRATIONS`, `QUO_API_KEY` and `CUSTOMER_REF_KEY`.
- Connector test kit: a Quo mock built from its docs, with call and message fixture builders.
