- Microsoft 365 Outlook connector (`@karatos/connectors`): reads one Exchange Online mailbox's Inbox, Sent Items and calendar through Microsoft Graph's deltas: five years of messages and appointments from five years back to a year ahead, then every change since the last sync. Messages become `communication.message_logged` (or `communication.message_removed`), with senders and recipients as the same correspondent references Gmail uses, and appointments `calendar.appointment_updated` or `calendar.appointment_cancelled`. Subjects, bodies, attachments, event titles, locations and meeting links are never kept. The app holds Exchange's Application Mail.ReadBasic and Application Calendars.Read roles for the one mailbox, and the client stops if its token carries any permission that isn't a read one; drafting replies and booking appointments are declared and turned off. Turn it on with `microsoft_outlook` in `ENABLED_INTEGRATIONS`, `MICROSOFT_TENANT_ID`, `MICROSOFT_OUTLOOK_CLIENT_ID`, `MICROSOFT_OUTLOOK_CLIENT_SECRET`, `MICROSOFT_OUTLOOK_MAILBOX` and `CORRESPONDENT_HASH_KEY`.
- Connector test kit: a Microsoft Graph mail and calendar delta mock built from Microsoft's reference, with fixture builders for messages and events.
