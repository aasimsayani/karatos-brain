- Zendesk connector (`@karatos/connectors`): imports five years of helpdesk tickets and customers from Zendesk's incremental exports, then reads only what changed on each sync. Tickets arrive as `communication.ticket_updated`, as Gorgias's do, with status, type, priority, channel, tags, requester, assignee, group, satisfaction rating and times, and customers (end users, not staff) as `customer.profile_updated` carrying the same customer references custom forms, Klaviyo, Quo and Gorgias make. It signs in with an OAuth refresh token an admin made with only `tickets:read` and `users:read`, stops if the token has a scope that writes, and keeps each rotated refresh token encrypted in the instance's database. Adding an internal note and tagging a ticket are declared behind the write gate and turned down. No subjects, comments, names or custom fields are kept. Turn it on with `zendesk` in `ENABLED_INTEGRATIONS`, `ZENDESK_SUBDOMAIN`, `ZENDESK_CLIENT_ID`, `ZENDESK_CLIENT_SECRET`, `ZENDESK_REFRESH_TOKEN` and `CUSTOMER_REF_KEY`.
- Connector test kit: a Zendesk mock built from its developer docs, with ticket and user fixture builders.
