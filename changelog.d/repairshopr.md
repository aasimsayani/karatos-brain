- RepairShopr connector (`@karatos/connectors`): imports five years of repair tickets, estimates and invoices, then the tickets and invoices that changed and the recent estimates on each later sync. They become `repair.ticket_updated`, `repair.estimate_updated` and `repair.invoice_updated` events, with status, dates, totals in cents and whether an invoice is paid. Customers are kept as their RepairShopr ID, never a name, email, phone or address, and ticket subjects, comments and invoice notes aren't kept. The token needs only the three List/Search permissions, and the client sends only GET requests to those lists; nothing is written back. Turn it on with `repairshopr` in `ENABLED_INTEGRATIONS`, `REPAIRSHOPR_API_TOKEN` and `REPAIRSHOPR_SUBDOMAIN`.
- Connector test kit: a RepairShopr API mock built from its docs, with ticket, estimate and invoice fixture builders.
