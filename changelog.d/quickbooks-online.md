- QuickBooks Online connector: reads the chart of accounts, customers, vendors, invoices, bills, payments and journal entries, six years back, then every change and deletion on the sync schedule. It is read-only even though Intuit has no read-only scope: only GET requests, and only SELECT queries. Customer and vendor emails, phones, addresses and tax IDs are left out. Turn it on with `quickbooks` in `ENABLED_INTEGRATIONS` and the `QUICKBOOKS_*` settings in `secrets/manifest.json`.
- Instance: keeps the QuickBooks refresh token Intuit rotates each day in its database, encrypted with a key derived from the app's client secret, so syncing keeps working across restarts.
- Connector test kit: a QuickBooks Online mock built from Intuit's docs, with token rotation, and fixture builders for each entity. `verifyQuickBooksWebhook` checks the signatures on CloudEvents webhooks.
- Nightly sandbox run: a QuickBooks Online check, which runs once the `SANDBOX_QUICKBOOKS_*` secrets are set.
