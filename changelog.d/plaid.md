- Plaid connector: reads one linked bank's accounts, balances and transactions, up to two years back, then every new, changed and removed transaction on the sync schedule, through `/transactions/sync`. Posted transactions arrive as `bank.transaction_imported` in the bank statements shape, so finance reads them like a statement. It is read-only: the client calls an allowlist of read endpoints only and refuses anything else before sending it, and transfers, Item removal and processor tokens are declared as writes behind the write gate. No account numbers, owner details, locations or payment details are kept. Turn it on with `plaid` in `ENABLED_INTEGRATIONS` and the `PLAID_*` settings in `secrets/manifest.json`.
- Connector test kit: a Plaid mock built from Plaid's API reference, with cursor paging, mid-loop mutation, rate limits and signed webhooks, and fixture builders for accounts and transactions. `verifyPlaidWebhook` checks the ES256 `Plaid-Verification` JWT, its age and the body hash.
- Nightly sandbox run: a Plaid check, which runs once the `SANDBOX_PLAID_*` secrets are set.
