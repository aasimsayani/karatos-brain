- Docusign connector (read-only): the account's envelopes with their recipients, as `agreement.updated` events with each agreement's kind, status and signing times, and who has yet to sign. It signs in with JWT Grant and never downloads a document. Recipients' email addresses only make keyed customer references; names, subjects, messages and decline reasons aren't kept. Connect deliveries are checked against the configuration's HMAC keys. Sending an envelope is declared behind the write gate and turned down.
