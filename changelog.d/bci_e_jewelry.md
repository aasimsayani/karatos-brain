- e-Jewelry for Business Central connector (`@karatos/connectors`): imports every item and vendor and five years of sales and purchase invoices through Business Central's API v2.0, then what changed on each sync. Items become `accounting.item_updated` with price, cost and units on hand, vendors `accounting.vendor_updated`, sales invoices `accounting.invoice_updated` and purchase invoices `accounting.bill_updated`, in the same shapes NetSuite, QuickBooks and Xero use. It signs in service to service with a Microsoft Entra app given read-only permission sets in Business Central, and sends only GETs marked `Data-Access-Intent: ReadOnly`. A customer is kept as Business Central's id only. Creating a draft sales quote and updating an item are declared behind the write gate and turned down. Turn it on with `bci_e_jewelry` in `ENABLED_INTEGRATIONS` and the `EJEWELRY_*` settings in `secrets/manifest.json`.
- Connector test kit: a Business Central mock built from Microsoft's docs, with item, vendor, sales invoice and purchase invoice fixtures.
