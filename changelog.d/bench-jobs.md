- The first manufacturing department, bench jobs (`bench`), in a new `@karatos/manufacturing` package on the same department contract as retail. A job moves through design and CAD, casting, stone setting, finishing and quality control to ready; each stage it enters is a `bench_job.stage_recorded` event with its time, the bench jeweler and the metal weighed in and out, and `bench_job.opened` says what the job is for, including the repair ticket or custom order it serves, whose promised date and description it then uses. Custom orders' own stages are followed too when no bench job names them. It raises jobs in one stage past its limit (`BENCH_STAGE_LIMIT_DAYS`, default design 7, casting 4, setting 4, finishing 3, QC 2 and on hold 14 days); jobs the store's own history of stage-to-ready times says will miss their promised date, and unlinked work already past it; stages and jewelers whose work quality control sends back 20% of the time or more over 90 days; and jobs that lost more metal across their stages than the tolerance (`BENCH_METAL_LOSS_PERCENT`, default 3%), with the metal lost above it valued at the latest spot price. Turn it on with `bench` in `ENABLED_DEPARTMENTS`. See docs/departments/bench.md.
- The POS CSV import reads bench exports (files named `bench`, `job bag`, `work order` or `production`): one row per stage, with Job, Stage and Date required and Jeweler, Metal In (g), Metal Out (g), QC Result, Sent Back To, Description, Promised, Repair Ticket, Custom Order and Metal optional. See docs/integrations/inventory_pos.md.
