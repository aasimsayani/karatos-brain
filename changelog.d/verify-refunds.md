- Sync verification checks refunds too: `refund_count` and `refund_total` for Shopify, Square and Stripe, matched record by record by refund id, on the store day each refund was made (not the sale's day), with the same tolerance, missing, extra and amount-differs rules as sales. Shopify and Square refunds are counted as the daily brief counts them: each `sale.returned` event once, at the sum of its returned items' refunds; Shopify's are read with the orders query and `read_orders`, Square's are the returns in completed orders from Search Orders (`ORDERS_READ`). Stripe's are the refunds list the connector already imports, counted when they succeeded. No new scope and no writes. A Shopify order with more refunds or refund line items than one read returns makes its system's refund metrics unavailable, naming the order. See [verifying synced numbers](docs/verify-sync.md).
- Connectors (`@karatos/core`): `VerifySource` gains an optional `unavailable` reason, so a connector can report one subject as unverifiable while the rest are still compared.
