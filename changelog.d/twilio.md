- Twilio connector: reads the store's texts, WhatsApp messages and calls from the account's logs as `communication.message_logged` and `communication.call_logged` events, through a restricted API key with read permissions only (the account's Auth Token is turned down). Message bodies are never kept, customers' numbers become keyed references (`TWILIO_CONTACT_KEY`) and the store's line is kept as its last four digits. Five years of history, checkpoints after every page, and each sync reaches two days back for late delivery receipts. No webhooks, since a number's webhook decides how the line answers. Sending texts and placing calls are declared behind the write gate and turned down. See docs/integrations.md.
- Clienteling flags callers who rang the store in the last seven days without getting through, until someone calls or texts them back.
