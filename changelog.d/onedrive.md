- OneDrive and SharePoint connector (read-only): one document library's files and folders as `document.file_updated` and `document.file_removed` events, the same ones Google Drive and Dropbox give, with each file's kind, MIME type, size, times and QuickXorHash. The historical import reads the drive's delta for five years of files; later syncs read only what changed, so edits, renames, moves and deletions are seen without listing again, and a 410 lists the library again. Graph is never asked for people, sharing or links, and names can be left out. The app is meant to hold `Sites.Selected` with the read role on one site, and the connector stops if its token carries any permission that isn't a read one. Paging links are checked and rebuilt, never fetched as given. Notifications carrying the subscription's clientState prompt a sync. Saving a report and making a subscription are declared behind the write gate and turned down.
