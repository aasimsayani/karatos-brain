- eBay connector (`@karatos/connectors`): imports the two years of orders eBay keeps through its Fulfillment API, then reads every order changed since the last sync. Paid orders give `sale.completed`, lines refunded in full give `sale.returned`, and every order gives `order.updated` with what eBay kept in fees, in the same shapes as Shopify and BigCommerce. Every access token is asked for with `sell.fulfillment.readonly` alone, its only API request is getOrders, and no buyer username, name, email address or address is kept. Setting a listing's quantity or price and adding tracking are declared behind the write gate, turned off, each needing approval. Turn it on with `ebay` in `ENABLED_INTEGRATIONS`, `EBAY_CLIENT_ID`, `EBAY_CLIENT_SECRET` and `EBAY_REFRESH_TOKEN`, and `EBAY_ENVIRONMENT=sandbox` for eBay's sandbox.
- Connector test kit: an eBay Fulfillment API and OAuth refresh mock built from its docs, with a fixture builder for orders.
- Nightly sandbox run: an eBay check against eBay's sandbox, which runs once its three `SANDBOX_EBAY_*` secrets are set.
