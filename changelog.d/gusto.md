- Gusto connector (`@karatos/connectors`): imports three years of one company's processed payrolls by check date, in windows of at most a year, with each payroll's totals and what each employee was paid (hours, overtime, commissions, bonuses and tips), then reads again from 45 days back on every sync. Employees are kept as Gusto's id only, with no names, pay rates, bank details or tax withholdings. The app holds only `payrolls:read`, and after every token refresh the client checks `token_info` and stops on another company or any scope beyond reading. Refresh tokens are replaced on every use, so the newest is saved first and kept sealed in the instance's database. `verifyGustoWebhook` checks `X-Gusto-Signature`. Turn it on with `gusto` in `ENABLED_INTEGRATIONS` and the `GUSTO_*` settings in `secrets/manifest.json`.
- Finance: payroll signals from Gusto, and a reminder to check a regular payroll more than 25% above the average of the three before it.
- Connector test kit: a Gusto mock built from its docs, with fixture builders for payrolls and employee compensations.
- Nightly sandbox run: a Gusto check against Gusto's demo environment, which runs once the `SANDBOX_GUSTO_*` secrets are set.
