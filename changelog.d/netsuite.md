- NetSuite connector (`@karatos/connectors`): imports every item and vendor and six years of sales orders, purchase orders, invoices and journal entries with SuiteQL, then what changed on each sync, and stock by location each time. Items become `accounting.item_updated`, vendors `accounting.vendor_updated`, and transactions `accounting.sales_order_updated`, `accounting.purchase_order_updated`, `accounting.invoice_updated` and `accounting.journal_entry_updated`, in the same shapes QuickBooks and Xero use where one exists; stock becomes `inventory.counted` for each item at each location. It signs in by OAuth 2.0 machine-to-machine with a certificate mapped to a view-only role, asks only for the `rest_webservices` scope, and sends only fixed SELECT queries. A vendor who is a person is never named. Creating a sales or purchase order is declared behind the write gate and turned down. Turn it on with `netsuite` in `ENABLED_INTEGRATIONS` and `NETSUITE_ACCOUNT_ID`, `NETSUITE_CLIENT_ID`, `NETSUITE_CERTIFICATE_ID` and `NETSUITE_PRIVATE_KEY`.
- Connector test kit: a NetSuite mock built from its docs, with item, vendor, transaction and stock fixtures.
