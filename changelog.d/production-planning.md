- Production planning (`production`), the second manufacturing department, reading what bench jobs records. A stage record can now carry `laborMinutes`, the bench minutes worked on it (the bench CSV export reads it from a Labor Minutes or Labor Hours column). It raises pieces whose labor, at each jeweler's hourly rate (`BENCH_LABOR_RATE`, default $45, with jewelers' own rates allowed), and metal at melt come to more than 60% of their repair estimate or custom order quote; alloys and jewelers whose metal loss rate over the last 30 days is a point or more above the 90 days before, with the extra loss valued at spot; and jewelers, or the whole bench, with more work queued than their hours over the next 7 days (`BENCH_DAILY_HOURS`, default 6 a day, on the store's working days), naming the promised jobs the queue pushes past their dates and what the hours over cost. Remaining work is planned from the store's own median bench minutes per stage and the stages its finished jobs went on to. Turn it on with `production` in `ENABLED_DEPARTMENTS`. See docs/departments/production.md.
