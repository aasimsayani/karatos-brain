- Birdeye connector (`@karatos/connectors`): imports five years of a business's reviews from every site Birdeye gathers them from, then every new or changed review, as the same `review.updated` events Yext's give. Birdeye keys can't be limited to reading, so the client sends Get Reviews only, with a body it builds itself; replying to a review is declared behind the write gate and turned down. No reviewer names, photos, customer records or custom fields are kept, and email addresses and phone numbers in review text are masked. Turn it on with `birdeye` in `ENABLED_INTEGRATIONS`, `BIRDEYE_API_KEY` and `BIRDEYE_BUSINESS_ID`.
- Connector test kit: a Birdeye mock built from its docs, with a review fixture builder.
