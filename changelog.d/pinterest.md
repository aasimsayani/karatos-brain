- Pinterest connector (`@karatos/connectors`): imports every Pin the store has made, with its title, the page it links to and its 90-day and lifetime metrics, the account's daily impressions, saves, Pin clicks and outbound clicks for the 90 days Pinterest keeps, and the 50 Pins saved most over the last 30 days, ranked. It connects with `boards:read`, `pins:read` and `user_accounts:read` only and stops if the token holds anything more, its only API requests are the Pin list and two analytics reads, and it keeps the newest refresh token sealed in the instance's database. Creating a Pin for a product is declared behind the write gate, turned off, needing approval. Turn it on with `pinterest` in `ENABLED_INTEGRATIONS`, `PINTEREST_CLIENT_ID`, `PINTEREST_CLIENT_SECRET` and `PINTEREST_REFRESH_TOKEN`.
- Connector test kit: a Pinterest API v5 mock built from Pinterest's OpenAPI description, with a fixture builder for Pins.
- Nightly sandbox run: a Pinterest check against a test business account, which runs once its three `SANDBOX_PINTEREST_*` secrets are set.
