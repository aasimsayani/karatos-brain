- Meta Ads connector (`@karatos/connectors`): imports two years of daily Facebook and Instagram ad spend, impressions, reach, clicks, purchases and purchase value for every campaign in one ad account, then reads the last two weeks again on every sync, as Meta keeps attributing purchases to past views and clicks. It gives the same `ads.campaign_reported` event as Google Ads. It connects with a system user token holding `ads_read` only and stops if the token holds anything more, its only API requests are the permissions check and the insights read for the configured ad account, and it never follows a link Meta returns. Pausing a campaign and changing its budget are declared behind the write gate, turned off, each needing approval. Turn it on with `meta_ads` in `ENABLED_INTEGRATIONS`, `META_ADS_ACCESS_TOKEN` and `META_ADS_ACCOUNT_ID`.
- Connector test kit: a Meta Marketing API insights mock built from its docs, with a fixture builder for campaign rows.
- Nightly sandbox run: a Meta Ads check against a sandbox ad account, which runs once its two `SANDBOX_META_ADS_*` secrets are set.
