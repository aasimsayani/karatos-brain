- Verkada store traffic connector (`@karatos/connectors`): imports two years of hourly people counts from each camera's counting line through Verkada's occupancy trends, then the hours since the last sync. Each hour becomes a `store.traffic_counted` event with how many people came in and went out, the camera and its site. The site's street address isn't kept. It signs in with a read-only API key, traded for a 30-minute token, and declares no writes. Turn it on with `verkada` in `ENABLED_INTEGRATIONS` and `VERKADA_API_KEY`, and `VERKADA_REGION=eu` for an organization in the EU.
- Connector test kit: a Verkada mock built from its docs, with token, camera and hourly count routes.
