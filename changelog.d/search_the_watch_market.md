- Search The Watch Market connector (`@karatos/connectors`): reads the market prices, days to sell and 13 weeks of price history for each watch reference the store follows. Each day's prices become a `watch_market.price_observed` event, and each week of history a `watch_market.price_week` event. It stays inside the plan's daily allowance and declares no writes. Turn it on with `search_the_watch_market` in `ENABLED_INTEGRATIONS`, `STWM_API_KEY` and `STWM_REFERENCES`.
- Connector test kit: a Search The Watch Market mock built from its API reference, with resolve, prices, days to sell and history routes.
