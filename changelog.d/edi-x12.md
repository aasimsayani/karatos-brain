- EDI with retail partners connector (`@karatos/connectors`): reads the X12 files in the folder where a VAN or SFTP mailbox keeps them, for two years of purchase orders (850), inventory (846) and sell-through (852) from partners, and the acknowledgments (855), ship notices (856) and invoices (810) the business sent back. Delimiters come from each interchange's ISA segment, and every control number and count is checked: a broken envelope stops the sync with the file named, and a document that can't be read is dead-lettered with its problems listed. An order delivered twice is stored once and a changed one as new. Names and addresses are never stored, only location codes. Sending 855, 856 and 810 is declared behind the write gate. Turn it on with `edi_x12` in `ENABLED_INTEGRATIONS` and `EDI_IMPORT_DIR`.
