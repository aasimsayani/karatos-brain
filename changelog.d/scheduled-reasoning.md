- Scheduled reasoning: the department rules (payout matching, stock agreement, customers awaiting a reply and the rest) now run on their own, so the daily brief and answers to questions no longer show stale or missing findings when nobody calls `POST /v1/reason`. A run starts after each sync round that brought new events, just before the daily brief is built (reusing a run that succeeded in the last 10 minutes, or waiting for one in progress), and at least every `REASON_INTERVAL_MINUTES` (default 60, 5 to 1440) otherwise. Runs never overlap: a trigger during a run queues one more, which later triggers share. A failed run is logged with its error's class name and a count, never a message, and tried again at the next trigger. `REASON_SCHEDULE=off` turns it off. `GET /v1/support/status` shows the last run's trigger, times, outcome and counts under `reasoning`. The brief's `sync.reasoning`, its last line, and answers about findings and sync health say when the findings were last refreshed, or that the last run failed, hasn't happened yet or is off. `POST /v1/reason` is unchanged. See docs/deployment/instances.md.
