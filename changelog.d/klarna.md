- Klarna connector (`@karatos/connectors`): imports five years of Klarna payouts, then each new one, with every sale, return, fee and commission behind it. Payouts become `payout.updated` events under the payment reference the bank statement shows, and their lines `balance.transaction_recorded` events with the Klarna order, capture or refund and the store's own order reference. Klarna keys can't be limited to settlements, so the client sends only GET requests to the payouts and settlement transactions lists; capturing and refunding an order are declared behind the write gate and turned down. Turn it on with `klarna` in `ENABLED_INTEGRATIONS` and `KLARNA_API_KEY`, or `KLARNA_USERNAME` and `KLARNA_PASSWORD`.
- Connector test kit: a Klarna Settlements API mock built from its docs, with payout and settlement transaction fixture builders.
- Nightly sandbox run: a Klarna check, which runs once the `SANDBOX_KLARNA_API_KEY` secret is set to a playground key.
