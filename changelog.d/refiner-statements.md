- Refiner and scrap settlement statements connector (`@karatos/connectors`): imports five years of settlement lines from any refiner's CSV exports, and from PDF statements for a refiner whose layout is in its profile, one folder per refiner. Each line has the lot, metal, weights in grams (read from gram, pennyweight or troy ounce columns, never by guessing the unit), assay, fine metal credited, price, credit, fees and net, and says whether its fine weight matches its weight times its assay. The same line in overlapping exports, or in a CSV and a PDF, is stored once, and a corrected statement replaces the lot's earlier version. Unreadable lines are dead-lettered with their file and row. Turn it on with `refiner_statements` in `ENABLED_INTEGRATIONS` and `REFINER_STATEMENTS_IMPORT_DIR`.
- Metals department: takes `refining.settlement_line_imported` lines, asks for a recheck when a lot is credited less fine metal than its assay says, and suggests a second quote when a lot paid under 90% of spot per fine ounce on the day it settled.
