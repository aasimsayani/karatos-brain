- Wix connector: reads one Wix site's eCommerce orders, Wix Stores products with their variants and stock (Catalog V3 or V1, detected each pass) and contacts, the whole catalog and contact list and five years of orders, then every change on the sync schedule. Sales, returns, products, stock and profiles arrive as the same events the Shopify, Square, Clover, WooCommerce and BigCommerce connectors send (`order.updated`, `sale.completed`, `sale.returned`, `product.updated`, `inventory.counted`, `customer.profile_updated`). It signs in as a self-managed Wix app rather than an API key, because an app's granted permissions can be read back and only apps receive webhooks. It stops if the app holds any Manage or write permission, and sends only eight listed method and path pairs (most Wix reads are POST queries). It pages with Wix's cursors on `updatedDate`, restarts a stream when Wix no longer takes a saved cursor, and waits out 429s. Contacts become references keyed with `CUSTOMER_REF_KEY`; no names, contact details, addresses or notes are kept. Turn it on with `wix` in `ENABLED_INTEGRATIONS` and the `WIX_*` settings in `secrets/manifest.json`.
- `verifyWixWebhook` checks a Wix webhook's JWT: RS256 only, against the app's public key, with `exp`, `nbf`, `iat` and `iss` when present, and the site's app instance. `WixReplayGuard` handles each event ID once within Wix's retry window.
- Connector test kit: a Wix mock built from Wix's docs, with app tokens and permissions, keyset cursors and both catalogs, and fixture builders for each record type.
- Nightly sandbox run: a Wix check, which runs once the `SANDBOX_WIX_*` secrets are set.
- Secrets manifest: `WIX_APP_ID`, `WIX_APP_SECRET`, `WIX_INSTANCE_ID`, `WIX_WEBHOOK_PUBLIC_KEY` and `CUSTOMER_REF_KEY`.
