- Added the "Any other mailbox (IMAP)" connector (read-only): five years of mail metadata from one mailbox's INBOX and Sent folder at any provider that offers IMAP, then new mail by UID, as the same events the Gmail connector gives: who wrote to whom, when and in which thread, with correspondent references in place of addresses and no bodies, attachments or subjects. It speaks IMAP4rev1 over implicit TLS with Node's own TLS (no STARTTLS or plaintext), signs in with an app password, opens folders with EXAMINE and fetches with BODY.PEEK, and can send seven commands and no others. A folder whose UIDVALIDITY changes is read again. Saving a draft reply and flagging a message are declared behind the write gate and turned down.
- Connector test kit: a scripted RFC 3501 IMAP server over an in-memory transport, with a message fixture builder, which answers BAD to and records any command a read-only client mustn't send.
