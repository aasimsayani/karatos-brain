- GIA Report Results connector (`@karatos/connectors`): looks up the GIA report behind each certificate number on the business's list once, with shape, carat weight, color, clarity, cut, polish, symmetry, fluorescence, inscription, proportions and report type, then keeps every report current through GIA's free changed-reports feed instead of paying for a second lookup. A number GIA has no report for is recorded once as `grading.report_unavailable`. The client sends two read queries only and never a mutation, sends the key to https gia.edu addresses only, caps lookups per sync (`GIA_MAX_LOOKUPS_PER_SYNC`, 100 by default), and stops on a used-up quota. Asset links, which expire after an hour, are not stored. Turn it on with `gia` in `ENABLED_INTEGRATIONS` and the `GIA_*` settings in `secrets/manifest.json`.
- Connector test kit: a GIA mock built from its developer docs, with a diamond report fixture builder and GIA's sandbox report numbers.
- Nightly sandbox run: a GIA check, which runs once the `SANDBOX_GIA_*` secrets are set.
