- Inventory (`@karatos/retail`): checks that the systems counting stock agree. Each store system's on-hand count (`inventory.counted`, from Katana, Acumatica JewelShop, Cin7 Core, NetSuite, the POS export, Square, Clover, Lightspeed, BigCommerce, WooCommerce, Wix, Etsy, Squarespace, Whatnot and WatchTraderHub) and each Shopify variant's quantity (`product.updated`) is kept as an `inventory.stock_level` signal. A new rule, `inventory.stock-agreement`, matches items by SKU without case, spaces, dashes or underscores, adds up each system's locations, and raises an item whose counts differ by more than 5% of the larger (any difference under 20 on hand), naming each system with its count and when it held. Counts more than 24 hours apart, or last known more than 7 days ago, are not compared. Three or more systems where one is out name that one; five or more items, and half or more of those two systems both count, that disagree at once are raised once for the systems. When the store's cost for the piece is known, the pieces in question carry a dollar figure the value ledger counts as protected. See [checking stock counts agree](docs/departments/README.md#checking-stock-counts-agree) for what each system gives and the limits.
