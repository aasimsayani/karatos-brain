- Stuller connector (`@karatos/connectors`): reads the invoices on the business's Stuller account from the last five years and records each one that is new or changed as `vendor_invoice.received`, with its lines, totals, status, tracking number and the metal rates it was priced at, and Stuller's current gold, silver, platinum and palladium prices as `vendor_metal_rate.published` when they change. The developer login is sent only to `api.stuller.com`. Ship-to addresses and tracking links aren't stored. Turn it on with `stuller` in `ENABLED_INTEGRATIONS` and the `STULLER_*` settings in `secrets/manifest.json`.
- Connector test kit: the Stuller mock can check the developer login and answer 401 to a wrong one.
