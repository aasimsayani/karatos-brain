- UPS tracking connector (read-only): follows each UPS tracking number on the business's list until the package is delivered or returned, as `shipment.tracked` events with a status every carrier maps to, UPS's own code and text, the latest scan time, the expected and delivered day, the service, whether a signature or UPS Premier was asked for, and the destination's state and country. Names, street addresses, postal codes, signatures and proof of delivery are never asked for or kept. It signs in with a UPS app's client id and secret, holding only the Tracking product, and a package that never moves is dropped after 120 days. The tracking logic is shared, so FedEx and USPS can follow.
