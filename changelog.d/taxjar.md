- TaxJar connector (`@karatos/connectors`): imports five years of the sales and refunds a store records in TaxJar, by 31-day spans for each source named in `TAXJAR_PROVIDERS` (`api` by default), then reads the last 31 days again on each sync, since TaxJar keeps no change time. Each becomes the same `tax.transaction_updated` event AvaTax transactions give, with the amount, shipping and sales tax in cents, the state and country, the exemption and the lines by SKU and tax code; the nexus regions become one `tax.nexus_updated` event. TaxJar tokens can't be limited to reading, so the client sends only its five GETs; recording a sale or refund is declared behind the write gate and turned down. Ship-to streets, cities and ZIP codes and line descriptions aren't kept, and the customer id becomes a keyed reference. Turn it on with `taxjar` in `ENABLED_INTEGRATIONS`, `TAXJAR_API_TOKEN` and `CUSTOMER_REF_KEY`.
- Connector test kit: a TaxJar mock built from its docs, with order and refund fixture builders.
