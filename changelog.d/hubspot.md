- HubSpot connector (`@karatos/connectors`): imports five years of contacts, companies, deals, tickets and logged calls, emails, meetings, notes and tasks from one HubSpot account through CRM search, pinned to the 2026-09 API version, then reads every change and deletion on the sync schedule. Search's 10,000-result cap is never reached: each new query starts from the last modified time read. It is read-only twice over: the token's scopes are checked with HubSpot before every sync, and one holding any write scope is refused, and the client sends only an allowlist of reads with search bodies it checked itself. Contacts become clienteling profiles with their name only and marketing consent off; deals, tickets and engagements keep a neutral CRM shape with no emails, phones, notes, bodies or recordings. `verifyHubSpotWebhook` checks v3 signatures and refuses deliveries more than five minutes old. Turn it on with `hubspot` in `ENABLED_INTEGRATIONS` and `HUBSPOT_ACCESS_TOKEN`.
- Connector test kit: a HubSpot mock built from HubSpot's docs, with fixture builders for each object.
- Nightly sandbox run: a HubSpot check, which runs once the `SANDBOX_HUBSPOT_ACCESS_TOKEN` secret is set.
