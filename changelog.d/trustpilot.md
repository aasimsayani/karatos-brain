- Trustpilot connector (`@karatos/connectors`): imports five years of the store's public Trustpilot reviews, then the reviews posted in the last 30 days again on each sync, so a reply or an edit shows up. Reviews become the same `review.updated` events Yext, Birdeye, Yotpo and Judge.me give, with the rating, an excerpt and title with email addresses and phone numbers masked, verification, and whether and when the store replied; reviews of two stars or fewer are marked for follow-up and unanswered ones as waiting on a reply. The trust score and review counts by rating become `review.score_updated` events. Reviewer names and locations aren't kept. The client uses only an application's API key, which reaches only Trustpilot's public endpoints; replying to a review and sending an invitation are declared behind the write gate and turned down. Turn it on with `trustpilot` in `ENABLED_INTEGRATIONS`, `TRUSTPILOT_API_KEY` and `TRUSTPILOT_BUSINESS_UNIT_ID`.
- Connector test kit: a Trustpilot mock built from its docs, with review and business unit fixtures.
