- Metals-API connector: gold, silver, platinum and palladium spot prices in dollars per troy ounce, as the metals department's `metal.price_updated` events. The first import reads a year of daily closing prices (`METALS_API_HISTORY_DAYS`, up to five years), 30 days and one metal per request, and resumes from the same stretches after a crash; each incremental sync reads the latest prices for all four metals in one request. Bad quotes are skipped, answers in another currency stop the sync, and the access key never appears in errors. Read-only, with a nightly sandbox check on a free account. See docs/integrations.md.
