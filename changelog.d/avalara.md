- Avalara AvaTax connector (`@karatos/connectors`): imports five years of transactions by id, then reads those changed since the last pass on each sync, resuming part way without skipping or repeating one. Each becomes a `tax.transaction_updated` event with its kind, status, totals in cents and the tax by state, county, city and special district from AvaTax's summary, for finance and compliance. A license key can't be limited to reading, so the client sends only Ping, the company lookup and the transaction list, with filters built from checked values, and never follows `@nextLink`; Ping must confirm the configured account first. Recording, committing, voiding, adjusting and refunding are declared behind the write gate. Names, emails, addresses, exemption certificate and business numbers, purchase orders, descriptions and lines aren't kept; the customer code becomes a keyed reference and an email the same customer references custom forms and Klaviyo make. Turn it on with `avalara` in `ENABLED_INTEGRATIONS` and the `AVALARA_*` settings in `secrets/manifest.json`.
- Connector test kit: an AvaTax mock built from its docs, with fixture builders for companies and transactions.
- Nightly sandbox run: an AvaTax check, which runs once the `SANDBOX_AVALARA_*` secrets are set.
