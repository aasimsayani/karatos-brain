- Clover connector (`@karatos/connectors`): imports five years of one merchant's orders, payments and refunds, with items, stock on hand and customers, then reads every change after that. Events use the shapes the Shopify, POS and Stripe connectors already use (`order.updated`, `sale.completed`, `sale.returned`, `payment.charge_updated`, `payment.refund_updated`, `product.updated`, `inventory.counted`, `customer.profile_updated`). Clover can't report an app's permissions, so the client sends only GET requests to its listed merchant paths on Clover's regional or sandbox hosts. It signs in with Clover's v2 expiring OAuth tokens (the rotating refresh token is saved first and sealed in the instance's database) or a merchant API token. Orders, payments and refunds are read 89 days at a time under Clover's 90-day cap, and pages continue from the last record's time so a record changed mid-pass can't make one be missed. Customers are kept only as references keyed with `CUSTOMER_REF_KEY`, and a card payment keeps only its card type and last four digits. Rate limits are waited out, then the sync resumes from its checkpoint. `verifyCloverWebhook` checks `X-Clover-Auth` in constant time, and `cloverWebhookChanges` drops stale notifications and `CloverWebhookDeduper` repeated ones. The scheduled sync runs it when `clover` is in `ENABLED_INTEGRATIONS`, and the nightly sandbox run can check it against a Clover test merchant
