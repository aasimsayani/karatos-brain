- Each instance can set its own store hours. `STORE_HOURS` takes days or day ranges and one or more 24-hour ranges per day, or `closed`, such as `mon-fri 10:00-18:00; sat 10:00-17:00; sun closed` or `tue-sat 10:00-13:00, 14:00-19:00`, on the clock of `STORE_TIME_ZONE`. A day left out is closed, times fall on the quarter hour, and a range past midnight is rejected with a note to split it at midnight. `STORE_CLOSED_DATES` lists holidays as `YYYY-MM-DD`, shut all day on the store's calendar, and `AWAITING_REPLY_HOURS` (default 4, 1 to 72) sets how many open hours a customer waits before clienteling's awaiting-reply rule raises them. The rule now counts these hours instead of a fixed Monday to Saturday, 10:00 to 18:00 in New York, which stays the default, so nothing changes when they are unset. The instance checks all three at startup and won't start with one it can't read, naming the setting and the part that's wrong. `GET /v1/support/status` shows what it read under `settings`. See docs/deployment/instances.md#store-hours.
