- Deputy connector (`@karatos/connectors`): imports every location, area and team member, and three years of shifts, timesheets and leave, then each one Deputy says changed since the last sync. They become `labor.location_updated`, `labor.area_updated`, `labor.employee_updated`, `labor.shift_updated`, `labor.timesheet_updated` and `labor.leave_updated` events, with hours and cost in cents by day, location and area. Team members are kept as their Deputy ID, never a name, birthday, address or contact details, and comments aren't kept. Deputy tokens can't be limited to reading, so the client sends only the Resource API search for those six records; publishing a schedule and approving a timesheet are declared behind the write gate and turned down. Turn it on with `deputy` in `ENABLED_INTEGRATIONS`, `DEPUTY_ACCESS_TOKEN` and `DEPUTY_INSTALL`.
- Connector test kit: a Deputy Resource API mock built from its docs, with location, area, team member, shift, timesheet and leave fixture builders.
