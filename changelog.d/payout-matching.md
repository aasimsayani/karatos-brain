- Finance (`@karatos/retail`): matches payment processors' payouts to the sales they pay for. Payouts (`payout.updated`) and the sales, refunds and fees they cover (`balance.transaction_recorded`, and Authorize.net charges and refunds that name their batch) are kept as `payout.record` and `payout.line` signals for Stripe, Klarna, Aurora Payments, Affirm and Authorize.net. A new rule, `finance.payout-match`, raises a payout paid short (by more than the larger of $1 and 0.5% of what it covers), a payout that failed or has no record, and sales still in no payout past the processor's normal schedule (late, or missing after 30 more days). Short and missing money carries a dollar figure the value ledger counts as protected. See [matching payouts to sales](docs/departments/README.md#matching-payouts-to-sales) for what each processor gives and the limits.
