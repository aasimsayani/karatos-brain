- POS exports (`inventory_pos`) read repair tickets: a file named like `repairs`, `service tickets` or `job envelopes` gives `repair.received` once per ticket and `repair.status_changed` once per status it reaches, so the repairs department sees tickets that are late, waiting for pickup or unclaimed. The promised day is read as the store's own calendar day, and status words map by keyword. See `docs/integrations/inventory_pos.md`.
- Demo store: Example Street Jewelers now exports its repair envelopes from the POS and runs on supplier invoices and memos, so the demo shows the two jeweler problems it was missing. It raises 18 recommendations: a late ring sizing, a chain waiting 52 days for pickup, a pearl restring unclaimed for 240 days, a memo diamond that sold and must be paid for, and that memo's missing System of Warranties statement, on top of the 13 before. The demo brain reasons over the last 1,000 signals so the earliest sources still count.
