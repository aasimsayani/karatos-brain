- Squarespace Commerce connector (`@karatos/connectors`): reads the whole catalog with each variant's price, sale price and stock, every stock line, and five years of orders, then the orders changed since the last sync, from a store's Commerce APIs. Products become `product.updated`, stock lines `inventory.counted` and orders `order.updated`, with customers under keyed references and no emails, names or addresses. It connects with an API key made with Read Only permission for Orders, Inventory and Products, and sends only GET requests; updating stock and fulfilling orders are declared and turned off. Turn it on with `squarespace` in `ENABLED_INTEGRATIONS` and `SQUARESPACE_API_KEY`.
- Connector test kit: a Squarespace Commerce APIs mock built from Squarespace's reference, with fixture builders for products, stock lines and orders.
