- Zoom connector (`@karatos/connectors`): imports six months of the account's past meetings from Zoom's meeting history report, a month at a time, with who joined each from its participant report, then reads the last two days again on each sync. Each meeting becomes an `appointment.held` event with its start, end, length, kind (bridal, repair and so on, read from the topic), how many joined, a keyed host reference, and the guests as the same keyed customer references Calendly bookings and custom forms make. Names and email addresses aren't kept, and the topic only when it names nobody; recordings, chat and summaries are never read, only whether the meeting used them. It signs in with a Server-to-Server OAuth app and won't use a token whose scopes could change anything. Scheduling a meeting is declared behind the write gate and turned down. Turn it on with `zoom` in `ENABLED_INTEGRATIONS`, `ZOOM_ACCOUNT_ID`, `ZOOM_CLIENT_ID`, `ZOOM_CLIENT_SECRET` and `CUSTOMER_REF_KEY`.
- Connector test kit: a Zoom mock built from its docs, with meeting and participant fixture builders.
