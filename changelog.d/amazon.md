- Amazon connector (`@karatos/connectors`): imports two years of Amazon orders through the Selling Partner API, counting paid ones as sales next to the store and website, with every sale, fee, refund and transfer Amazon posts to the seller's account and each SKU's stock in Amazon's warehouses. Its only API requests are three GETs and the Login with Amazon token refresh, and it never asks for the buyer or the address. Changing a listing's quantity or price is declared behind the write gate, turned off, needing approval. Turn it on with `amazon` in `ENABLED_INTEGRATIONS` and `AMAZON_SP_CLIENT_ID`, `AMAZON_SP_CLIENT_SECRET` and `AMAZON_SP_REFRESH_TOKEN`, plus `AMAZON_SP_MARKETPLACE_ID`, `AMAZON_SP_REGION` and `AMAZON_SP_ENVIRONMENT` when needed.
- Connector test kit: an Amazon Selling Partner API mock built from Amazon's docs, covering the order search, finance transactions, FBA stock and the Login with Amazon refresh, with fixture builders for orders, transactions and stock.
- Nightly sandbox run: an Amazon check against the SP-API sandbox, which runs once its three sandbox secrets are set.
