- Finance department (`@karatos/retail`): reads bank account balances (`bank.account_updated`, from Plaid) and sales invoices from the books (`accounting.invoice_updated` and deleted invoices, from QuickBooks and Xero). Each balance becomes a `bank.balance` signal and each invoice a `receivable.invoice` signal with what is still owed, so the console can show cash position and receivables. A new recommendation asks for follow-up when $500 or more in US dollar invoices is more than 30 days past due.
- Instance API (`@karatos/server`): `GET /v1/signals` reads the latest signals, filtered by up to 10 kinds (`?kind=bank.balance,receivable.invoice`), up to 1,000 at a time.
- Memory (`@karatos/core`, `@karatos/store-postgres`): `recentSignals` takes an optional list of kinds, backed by a new index on signals by kind (migration `20261027000001_signals_by_kind.sql`).
