- Added the JM Shipping Solution connector (read-only): the insured parcels a store ships through Jewelers Mutual, from the JM Shipping Solution API, as shipments with their carrier, service, declared and invoice values, their latest tracking, each insurance claim's status and amounts, and each reconciled carrier charge; the historical import reads five years, and later syncs keep the last 60 days current. It reads with the key and password JM issues the store, the client only sends GET requests, and no names, companies, street addresses, postal codes, phone numbers or package contents are kept. Buying labels, cancelling shipments, filing claims and disputing charges are declared behind the write gate and turned down.
