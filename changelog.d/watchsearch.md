- TheWatchSearch connector: reads the WhatsApp chat exports from the store's dealer groups, since TheWatchSearch publishes no API or export, and turns each offer into a `watch_market.offer_seen` event for buying: brand, reference, selling or looking for, price and currency, year, condition and set. It reads iPhone and Android exports in either date order, and the trade's shorthand for brands and prices. Dealers are kept as keyed references (`WATCHSEARCH_DEALER_KEY`), and the messages' own words are never stored. Read-only, with no vendor contact. See docs/integrations.md.
- Buying recommends offering a watch when three or more dealers have asked for its reference in the last 14 days, with the lowest price other dealers in the groups are asking.
