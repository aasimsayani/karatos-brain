- Acumatica JewelShop connector (`@karatos/connectors`): reads every stock item, with what each warehouse has on hand, on every sync, and imports five years of sales orders, then the orders changed since the last sync, from the instance's Default REST endpoint. Stock items become `product.updated`, warehouse lines `inventory.counted` and orders `order.updated`, with customers under keyed references. It signs in with a connected application and a KaratOS user whose role can only view stock items and sales orders; the client sends only GET requests for those two besides signing in and out, and declares no writes. JewelShop's memo and consignment screens aren't on the Default endpoint yet. Turn it on with `acumatica_jewelshop` in `ENABLED_INTEGRATIONS` and the five `ACUMATICA_*` settings.
- Connector test kit: an Acumatica contract-based REST API mock built from Acumatica's guide, with fixture builders for stock items and sales orders.
