- Calendly connector (`@karatos/connectors`): imports two years of appointments and the year ahead, such as bridal consultations, custom design meetings, repair drop-offs, appraisals and pickups, one per invitee, then reads the last 30 days to a year ahead on each sync for moves, cancellations and no-shows. Appointments arrive as `appointment.booked`, `appointment.canceled` and `appointment.no_show`, with their kind, times, hosts, kind of place, payment and UTM source. The client sends only its three listed GETs, with ids and page tokens it checked, and never follows a `next_page` link. Invitees are read again only for events that changed or started in the last 14 days, so a free plan's rate limit is enough; 429s are waited out from `X-RateLimit-Reset`. Without owner or admin rights it reads the token owner's own events. Names, emails, phone numbers, answers, notes, cancellation reasons and addresses aren't kept; the email and text reminder number make the same customer references custom forms and Klaviyo make. Booking, cancelling and marking no-shows are declared behind the write gate. No webhooks, which need a paid plan. Turn it on with `calendly` in `ENABLED_INTEGRATIONS` and `CALENDLY_ACCESS_TOKEN` from `secrets/manifest.json`.
- Connector test kit: a Calendly mock built from its docs, with fixture builders for scheduled events and invitees.
- Nightly sandbox run: a Calendly check, which runs once the `SANDBOX_CALENDLY_ACCESS_TOKEN` secret is set.
