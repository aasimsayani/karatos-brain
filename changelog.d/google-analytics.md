- Google Analytics 4 connector (`@karatos/connectors`): imports two years of daily traffic from one GA4 property (sessions, users, new users, engaged sessions, key events, purchases and revenue by channel and by source and medium) and daily product views, adds to cart, purchases and item revenue, then reads the last few days again on every sync, as GA4 keeps revising them. It connects with `analytics.readonly` only and stops if the token holds more, and its only API request is `runReport` for the configured property, built from two fixed reports. Reports are totals by day, so no visitor is named, and a source, medium or item name holding an @ is dropped. There is nothing to write. Turn it on with `google_analytics` in `ENABLED_INTEGRATIONS`, `GOOGLE_ANALYTICS_REFRESH_TOKEN`, `GOOGLE_ANALYTICS_PROPERTY_ID` and the shared Google OAuth client.
- Connector test kit: a GA4 Data API mock built from its docs, with fixture builders for traffic and item rows.
- Nightly sandbox run: a Google Analytics 4 check against Google's public demo account, which runs once its four `SANDBOX_GOOGLE_ANALYTICS_*` secrets are set.
