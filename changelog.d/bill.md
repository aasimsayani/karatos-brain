- BILL connector (read-only): reads supplier bills with their due dates, balances, payment and approval status, the vendors a business pays, and the payments it sends with the bills each paid, from BILL's v3 API, as the same `accounting.*` events QuickBooks and Xero give. It signs in with a developer app made for accounting data only, which can't create payments or change funding accounts, and keeps no vendor contact details, tax ids, bank details or memos.
