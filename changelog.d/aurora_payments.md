- Aurora Payments connector (`@karatos/connectors`): imports five years of card and ACH settlement batches from Flute, as Aurora Payments is now called, each with its transactions, then on each later sync the batches from the week before the last one, so a batch that settles later is read again. Batches become `payout.updated` events with the processor's batch number, sales, refunds and net in cents and whether they've settled; transactions become `balance.transaction_recorded` events under their batch with type, status, payment method, tax, tip and surcharge. No customer name, email, phone or card digits are kept. A merchant's own Flute key can reach every endpoint, so the client sends only the token request and those two lists; refunding and voiding are declared behind the write gate and turned down. Turn it on with `aurora_payments` in `ENABLED_INTEGRATIONS`, `AURORA_CLIENT_ID` and `AURORA_CLIENT_SECRET`, and `AURORA_ENVIRONMENT=sandbox` for a sandbox key.
- Connector test kit: a Flute (Aurora Payments) API mock built from its docs, with settlement batch and transaction fixture builders.
- Nightly sandbox run: an Aurora Payments check against Flute's sandbox (`SANDBOX_AURORA_PAYMENTS_CLIENT_ID`, `SANDBOX_AURORA_PAYMENTS_CLIENT_SECRET`).
