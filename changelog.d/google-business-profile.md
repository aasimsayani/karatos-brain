- Google Business Profile connector (`@karatos/connectors`): reads the account's locations, two years of Google reviews and daily metrics, then the new and edited reviews and the last week's metrics on each sync. Locations become `location.updated`, reviews `review.updated` in the shape Yext and Trustpilot give (rating, masked excerpt, reply status, follow-up flag for two stars or fewer), and each location's day `analytics.local_listing_reported` with Maps and Search views, calls, direction requests, website clicks, messages and bookings. The connection holds `business.manage` alone, the only scope Google offers, and the client sends only GETs. A reviewer's name and photo are never kept. Replying to a review is declared behind the write gate and turned down. Turn it on with `google_business_profile` in `ENABLED_INTEGRATIONS` and `GOOGLE_BUSINESS_PROFILE_REFRESH_TOKEN` and `GOOGLE_BUSINESS_PROFILE_ACCOUNT_ID`, with the shared Google OAuth client.
- Connector test kit: a Google Business Profile mock built from Google's docs, with location and review fixtures and daily metrics.
