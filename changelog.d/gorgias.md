- Gorgias connector (`@karatos/connectors`): imports five years of helpdesk tickets and every message on them, then reads only the tickets that changed on each sync, since Gorgias lists tickets by their last change. Tickets arrive as `communication.ticket_updated` with status, channel, tags, assignee, satisfaction score and times, and messages as the same `communication.message_logged` events Quo's and Gmail's give. It signs in as a Gorgias app connected with `tickets:read` and stops if the token has a scope that writes; adding an internal note and tagging a ticket are declared behind the write gate and turned down. No message text, subjects, attachments or customer names are kept, and customers' emails become the same customer references custom forms, Klaviyo, Quo and AvaTax make. Turn it on with `gorgias` in `ENABLED_INTEGRATIONS`, `GORGIAS_DOMAIN`, `GORGIAS_CLIENT_ID`, `GORGIAS_CLIENT_SECRET`, `GORGIAS_REFRESH_TOKEN` and `CUSTOMER_REF_KEY`.
- Connector test kit: a Gorgias mock built from its developer docs, with ticket and message fixture builders.
