- Xero connector (`@karatos/connectors`): imports six years of the chart of accounts, contacts, invoices and bills, credit notes, payments, bank transactions and manual journals from one Xero organisation, then reads every change after that with `If-Modified-Since`, voided and deleted records included. The connection uses Xero's granular read scopes only, and the client refuses an access token carrying any scope that could change data. Refresh tokens rotate on every use, so the newest is saved first and kept sealed in the instance's database. Minute rate limits are waited out; a daily limit stops the sync until the next run. `verifyXeroWebhook` checks webhook signatures. Contact details beyond names aren't kept. The scheduled sync runs it when `xero` is in `ENABLED_INTEGRATIONS`, and the nightly sandbox run can check it against the Demo Company
